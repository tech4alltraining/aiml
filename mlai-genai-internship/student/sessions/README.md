## What every session contains

```text
🎯 Learning outcomes     what you will be able to do afterwards
🧠 Analogy               the everyday comparison for each idea
📘 Examples              2-3 worked examples per topic, all runnable
✏️ Practice              5 short exercises per topic, with solutions
❓ MCQs                  multiple-choice checks, with answers and why
🎯 Tasks                 2-3 longer pieces for after the session
```

## How to work through a session

1. Read the session page for the idea and the analogy.
2. Open the notebook and run the examples.
3. Do the five practice exercises **before** looking at the solutions.
4. Answer the MCQs from memory. Anything wrong, go back to that topic.
5. Do the tasks in your own time.

> **If you get stuck for more than ten minutes**, look at the solution for *that step only*, understand it, close it, and continue.
